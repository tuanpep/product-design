Next, next gate, continue, proceed, approve, yes to a summary, or the next phase by name opens that phase now. Do not ask another discovery, confirmation, or level question, and do not keep Level understand or Next gate repeat discovery. Record missing facts as unknown and exclude unsafe behavior. Do not run technical design; that is `/technical-design`, only after Specify when this message opens it. Apply references/discovery-interview.md for new briefs, interviews, advances or changed core premises. A new brief without advance remains Understand, even for advise or Full; sufficient context needs no interview. Bare "no questions" without continuation returns only discovery summary, gaps and research plan. Advise recommends inside the open phase, never human approval.

Use relevant references/learning-loop.md sections for actual synthesis, evaluation or supplied results, not every task.
