---

Develop or revise a product design for the user's request. Load the design-product skill and follow it, including the playbook it selects and every principle leaf it names. If the user says advise, or asks you to suggest the solution and design without waiting for a decision, run advise mode: read references/advise-mode.md from the design-product skill and do not stop for a level, a layout, or any other decision. Otherwise, on a new idea, if the user did not name a level, stop and ask whether to run Understand, Design documents, Sketch, Prototype, or Full before any design work.
