Develop or revise a product design for the user's request. Load the design-product skill and follow it, including the playbook it selects and every principle leaf it names. Run one phase, then stop, unless this message asks to go next: Understand, then Shape, then Specify. Next, next gate, continue, proceed, approve, yes to a summary, or the next phase by name opens that phase now. Do not ask another discovery, confirmation, or level question, and do not keep Level understand or Next gate repeat discovery. Record missing facts as unknown. Do not run technical design; that is `/technical-design`, and only after Specify when this message asks to go next, approves it, or asks to brainstorm it. First apply references/discovery-interview.md. A new brief that does not ask to continue is interviewed in Understand, even for advise or Full. A bare "no questions" with no request to continue returns only a discovery summary, gaps, and research plan, then stops. If the user says advise, read references/advise-mode.md and recommend inside the open phase.

Use references/learning-loop.md to connect sources, synthesis, opportunity choice, alternatives, early test planning, and supplied results to decisions. Match artifacts to the research question and declared output level; do not mistake a complete design package for validation or approval.
