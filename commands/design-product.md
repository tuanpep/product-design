description: Develop or revise an evidence-labeled product design from an idea
---

Develop or revise a product design for the user's request. Load the design-product skill and follow it, including the playbook it selects and every principle leaf it names. On a new idea, if the user did not name a level, stop and ask whether to run Understand, Design documents, Sketch, Prototype, or Full before any design work.
