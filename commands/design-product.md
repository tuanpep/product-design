Develop or revise a product design for the user's request. Load the design-product skill and follow it, including the playbook it selects and every principle leaf it names. First apply references/discovery-interview.md: missing core context requires an interview in Understand, even for advise or Full, and confirmation before first Shape when an interview was triggered. If questions are explicitly forbidden, provide only discovery summary, gaps, and research plan, then stop. If the user says advise, read references/advise-mode.md and, after the context gate passes, do not stop for level or layout decisions. Otherwise, on a new idea with sufficient context but no named level, ask whether to run Understand, Design documents, Mockup, or Full before Shape.

Use references/learning-loop.md to connect sources, synthesis, opportunity choice, alternatives, early test planning, and supplied results to decisions. Match artifacts to the research question and declared output level; do not mistake a complete design package for validation or approval.
