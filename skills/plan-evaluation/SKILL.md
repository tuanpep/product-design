# Plan Evaluation

Read [the evidence contract](../design-product/references/evidence-and-decisions.md).

1. Name the assumption whose failure would reverse the recommendation.
2. Design the smallest ethical test that could distinguish it: who needs to take part, what they are asked to do, what will be observed, and which results would support, weaken, or reverse the recommendation.
3. Say whether the check is user-based or inspection-only. A sketch, heuristic pass, or automated scan is inspection. It cannot establish usability, accessibility, or that the need is real.
4. State what the test leaves unexamined, including people who are not in the session.

Record a plan as a plan. Do not invent participants, quotes, task times, or outcomes. If a real session is supplied, classify it as **observed** and record where, when, and what happened.

**Reply:** the assumption, the test, what result would reverse the recommendation, and what the test does not cover.
