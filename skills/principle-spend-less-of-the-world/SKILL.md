# Spend Less of the World

When a real choice changes energy, duplication, retained data, or how often people must repeat work, prefer the option that still meets the outcome at a lower lasting cost.

- This never justifies failing the outcome, excluding someone, or hiding harm
- If there is no real difference in lasting cost, do not invent one
