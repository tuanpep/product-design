# Review research basis and adaptation boundaries

External pages read 2026-10-22:

- [NASA technical assessment](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-7-technical-assessment/): purpose, entry/success criteria, findings and action resolution. Adopt scoped review, not NASA ceremony or a compliance claim.
- [NASA design solution definition](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-4-design-solution-definition/): requirements/constraints and distinction between design review and implemented-product verification/validation.
- [NASA requirements management](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-2-requirements-management/): bidirectional semantic traceability, change impact and dependent-artifact consistency.
- [Anthropic agent evaluations](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents): suitable deterministic/model/human checks, grade outcomes and traces, balanced cases, repeated isolated trials and calibration against human judgment. Agent evaluation is not product end-user validation.
- [GOV.UK sharing research findings](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/sharing-user-research-findings): evidence, significance and next actions that change decisions.

Read-only standalone review, authorized repair boundaries, finding classifications and three verdicts are plugin adaptations, not prescribed verbatim. Efficiency and accuracy require recorded installed-host evaluation; instruction presence and structural passes do not establish behavior.
