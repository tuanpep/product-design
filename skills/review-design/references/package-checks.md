# Conditional package integrity checks

Load only sections relevant to supplied artifacts or readiness claims. Legitimate deferral under the output standard is not a missing artifact; supplied out-of-phase artifacts still need review against the claims they actually make. Do not use this checklist to raise a cap or open a phase.

## Screen and diagram handoffs

- Every declared screen/business view in `screens.md` has a frame and traces to a need/task, plus a story when stories are in scope. Walk initiation, approval, execution, confirmation, tracking, completion, consequential branches and role handoffs.
- Flag receiving/approval forms hidden in annotations, false serial dependencies, setup mixed into daily work, generic success frames that skip decisions and missing populated collections/return navigation for repeated-record tasks.
- Do not demand routine empty/loading/partial/error/denied/success/offline boards. Preserve safety/recovery in concise notes or consequential exception views. Legacy declared States still require coverage until the owning spec changes; partial learning material does not excuse incomplete canonical handoffs.
- Compare diagrams with owning text; apply close-flow, size and [edge rules](../../drawio/references/drawio-file.md#relationship-lines). Inspect [layout fidelity](../../mockup-drawio/references/product-layouts.md#handoff-audit) against the recorded baseline when relevant. Valid XML or attractive colors cannot prove wrapping, rendering or product fidelity. Report unopened/inaccessible diagrams as unverified.

## Build and technical artifacts

- Build diagrams agree with domain/lifecycle/integration owners. Must stories have concrete Given, When, Then criteria and checks when stories are in scope. Open questions retain accountable owners or Unknown, proposed reversible defaults and visible blockers.
- When Technical is opened, supplied or claimed complete, included views are filled, omissions have reasons and correspondences name a single owning ID. Full alone does not demand technical completion; distinguish Specify handoff from technical readiness.
- Service operations trace meaningfully to stories. Contract fields deliberately map to business meaning rather than blindly copying tables. Load the schema-separation principle before citing it as a violation.
- Architecturally significant choices need adequate rationale even if reversible. Load the decision-record principle before citing a violation. Apply the [correctness/change-safety review](../../design-product/references/technical-design-format.md#correctness-and-change-safety-review); matching IDs/statuses and valid schemas do not prove enforcement or feasibility.

## Content when supplied or affected

Apply [the content contract](../../design-product/references/content-quality.md). Report four checks separately: clarity, business accuracy, journey/channel coverage and cross-artifact consistency, with failures or not applicable. Check reader/language/useful first sentence and terms; pending/completed/unknown results, permitted recovery and supported promises; relevant actors/screens/off-screen/support handoffs without invented views; one M catalogue, variables, rule/lifecycle references, translations and mockup instances. Check sensitive previews and destination access. After Specify/Technical changes, require reconciliation or an exact stale-file/owner/follow-up report. Useful prose explains decisions, not just headings. Inspection is not participant comprehension evidence.

## Triggered assessments

When barriers, processing harms or consequential quality targets are present, load the relevant [accessibility planning](../../design-product/references/accessibility-planning.md), [privacy-risk](../../design-product/references/privacy-risk.md) or [quality-scenario](../../design-product/references/quality-scenarios.md) guidance. Check existing owners, sourced/proposed/unknown targets, meaningful scenarios, planned versus performed evaluation and limitations. No new report or phase is required; security assessment alone cannot establish privacy safety and static/automated inspection cannot establish full accessibility. Scope the finding's readiness impact rather than demanding implementation results during design exploration.
