# Behave Predictably and Recoverably

Once a direction exists, judge the interaction with the seven principles of ISO 9241-110:2020. Nielsen's heuristics are a practical inspection of the same layer. Engagement never overrides the other six.

- **Suitable for the task.** Functions follow the task, not the technology chosen to perform it
- **Self-descriptive.** What it can do, what is happening, and what it means are visible without extra steps
- **Conformant with expectations.** Behaviour is predictable from this context and its conventions. The same thing means the same thing. Depart from a shared pattern only when this context requires it, and say why
- **Learnable.** People can find, try, and remember the path. Help is not a substitute for a clearer task
- **Controllable.** People can pause, go back, change their mind, and influence pace and sequence. Individualization must not become a burden on people who will never configure it
- **Robust to use error.** Prevent predictable mistakes, tolerate ones that still happen, and offer a recovery in the user's words
- **Engaging only in support of the six above.** Motivation and trust may not disguise a missing capability, manufacture urgency, or override a higher principle
