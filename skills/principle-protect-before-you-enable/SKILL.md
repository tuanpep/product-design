# Protect Before You Enable

Do not make a task easier in a way that hides a risk, traps a person, or spends their trust. Harm includes safety, privacy, being unable to recover, and being led to act on a false belief.

- A convenient path that does one of these loses to a slower path that does not
- This outranks outcome, scope, simplicity, interaction quality, cost, and polish
- Name the harm you refused, and the slower path you kept because of it
- A sentence that says the design is safe is not evidence that it is
