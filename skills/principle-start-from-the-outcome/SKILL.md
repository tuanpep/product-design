# Start from the Outcome

Design for the goal and the people who have it. A requested feature, screen, or technology is not a need.

- What someone asks for is **given**, not an observed need
- In ISO 9241-11, usability is effectiveness, then efficiency, then satisfaction, for specified users in a specified context
- Effectiveness outranks speed and delight
- Keep the proposed feature distinct from the need until evidence connects them
