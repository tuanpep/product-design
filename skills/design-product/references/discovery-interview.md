# Discovery interview and sufficient-context gate

This is the canonical context/advance policy for every mode and level. Reuse supplied facts; a short concrete brief can be sufficient and a long feature list insufficient. Clarification establishes intent/context, not end-user validation.

## Establish enough context

Identify actor/context, concrete task/trigger/current workflow, friction/consequence and source, observable desired outcome, decision and distinct user/business-service outcomes, first scope/priorities and direction-invalidating constraints. Include consequential secondary actors, assistance/access barriers and offline handoffs. A hypothetical scenario explicitly agreed by the human is an exploration premise, not research evidence. Never invent core facts, causal certainty, quotes or metrics.

Without an advance request stay in Understand. For consequential gaps ask 1–3 focused open questions, preferably one main question about a concrete case, reusing answers. Ask only when the answer could change framing, safe scope or next action. Missing nonessential metrics, preferences or owner names do not require more turns. For an already sufficient brief skip the interview. After an interview, summarize once without a separate confirmation question or duplicating chat into a new document. Enough context alone does not grant a later phase; finish the open phase and stop unless this message advances it.

If the person does not know, offer an explicitly agreed hypothetical premise or real research plan; never silently choose one. Do not endlessly paraphrase an unanswered question. Use [detailed methods/examples](discovery-method.md) only when conducting an interview or resolving a genuine method ambiguity.

## When they ask to leave Understand

An advance request opens the next requested phase in this message, overriding the interview, pending summary, Understand cap and `Next gate: repeat discovery`. Requests include next, next gate, next phase, continue, proceed, go ahead, move on, approval, or naming Shape/screens/mockup/Specify/Technical in that context. Yes/that's right/looks good in response to a summary or offer to continue also advance. After an interview has started, stop asking/no more questions advance. Bare no questions on a new brief without continuation does not: give only discovery summary, gaps and research plan, then stop.

Do not ask another discovery, confirmation, level or approval-versus-brainstorm question after advance. If Understand is unwritten, record known facts/unknowns then enter Shape. Unnamed Shape cap becomes Mockup; clear an Understand cap. Preserve other explicit caps. Record the request as an attributed human decision; brainstorming remains proposed. Problem/context brainstorming stays in Understand; solution/screens brainstorming opens Shape, slice/spec brainstorming opens Specify. Clarify an ambiguous brainstorm only if no advance already settles it.

Advance changes activity, not evidence or approval. Missing facts stay unknown, not invented defaults. Exclude unsafe behavior where safety/privacy/access is unknown; if the capability depends entirely on unresolved authorization/safety, consider only safe alternatives or the investigation needed. Nonblocking reversible choices in an open later phase may have proposed defaults with owners. A direction-invalidating unknown blocks affected delivery readiness even when exploration can proceed.

## While blocked or revising

Without advance, do not compare solutions or write to-be diagrams, screens, mockups or build specs. A discovery proposal may retain stable needs/constraints, current-workflow notes, gaps and research plan with discovery/repeat-discovery headers. No mandatory persona, permission matrix, diagram, separate evidence document or plan per minor gap. Advise/Full do not supply missing context or open a phase.

On revision reopen only affected core-premise gaps, not cosmetic/copy edits or unchanged facts; explicit advance still ends interviewing. Apply corrections without asking to confirm them again. A specific correction responding to an interview summary opens Shape, as does an accompanying advance; if it removes a core fact or remains consequentially ambiguous without advance, ask one focused follow-up instead. Preserve ambiguity as unknown when advance ends clarification. Preserve unaffected human work, withdraw stale dependent readiness within authorized scope and report remaining stale files. Retain source applicability, contradictions and limitations; stakeholder accounts are given, not directly observed user research.
