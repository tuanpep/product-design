# Product design principles

Use these to choose between options. They are decision rules. The workflow says how to learn. The evidence contract says how to label a claim. A principle in the proposal is a commitment, not a result.

Load only leaves that could change a choice, resolve a conflict or support an explicit citation. Cite only a principle whose `SKILL.md` you read this session; routine application of safeguards does not require loading every linked leaf. Keep all public principle names available for focused use. The leaves paraphrase [ISO 9241-210:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/77520.html), the [Design Council](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/), the [GOV.UK Government Design Principles](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/government-design-principles) (updated 2 April 2025), [ISO 9241-110:2020](https://www.iso.org/standard/75258.html), and, for the data-design principle, FIPS PUB 184 together with the British Columbia logical-model rule of third normal form. This index is not a claim of compliance.

## Precedence

Apply the first principle that can settle the conflict. A lower one never overrides a higher one.

1. [Protect before you enable](../../principle-protect-before-you-enable/SKILL.md) — harm, traps or spent trust.
2. [Start from the outcome](../../principle-start-from-the-outcome/SKILL.md) — a feature, screen or technology is mistaken for the goal.
3. [Include the hardest realistic case](../../principle-include-the-hardest-case/SKILL.md) — the polished path excludes people who need the outcome.
4. [See the whole service, then do less](../../principle-see-the-whole-service/SKILL.md) — choosing build, reuse, process change or stop.
5. [Take on the hard work](../../principle-take-on-the-hard-work/SKILL.md) — apparent simplicity transfers exceptions to users.
6. [Behave predictably and recoverably](../../principle-behave-predictably/SKILL.md) — interaction, reversibility and recovery choices.
7. [Spend less of the world](../../principle-spend-less-of-the-world/SKILL.md) — energy, duplication, retained data or repeated work.

The next eleven govern the quality of the specification and its technical design. They never override the seven above.

8. [Design every state](../../principle-design-every-state/SKILL.md) — meaningful lifecycle, integration and screen behavior, not invented state boards.
9. [Make it testable](../../principle-make-it-testable/SKILL.md) — observable requirements, meaningful condition/outcome and boundary coverage.
10. [One source of truth](../../principle-one-source-of-truth/SKILL.md) — duplicated terms, rules, states or messages.
11. [Decide the reversible](../../principle-decide-the-reversible/SKILL.md) — a builder would guess a local choice; blockers still need owners.
12. [Use real content](../../principle-use-real-content/SKILL.md) — realistic examples, copy and mockups.
13. [Choose the view for the concern](../../principle-choose-the-view/SKILL.md) — a template creates an unnecessary view or misses a real concern.
14. [Keep the schemas apart](../../principle-keep-the-schemas-apart/SKILL.md) — conceptual, internal and external representations differ; map intentionally.
15. [Write the contract first](../../principle-write-the-contract-first/SKILL.md) — consumers would infer an interface from screens or tables.
16. [Record the decision](../../principle-record-the-decision/SKILL.md) — architecturally significant choices, including reversible precedents, compatibility/security/operational boundaries.
17. [Settle the logical model](../../principle-settle-the-logical-model/SKILL.md) — types, indexes or product choices precede identifiers/cardinality/normalization.
18. [Prefer the standard method](../../principle-prefer-the-standard-method/SKILL.md) — custom HTTP actions/errors replace adequate standard semantics.

Polish, novelty, and satisfaction yield to all of the above. Name the principle that decided the trade-off and the principle it cost. If the evidence cannot support the higher principle, the gap stays unknown.

"Make things open" is a GOV.UK operating rule for public work. Apply it when the product is a public service or the human asked for it.
