# Product design principles

Use these to choose between options. They are decision rules. The workflow says how to learn. The evidence contract says how to label a claim. A principle in the proposal is a commitment, not a result.

Read the leaf skill in full for any principle you apply. Cite only a principle whose `SKILL.md` you read this session. The leaves paraphrase [ISO 9241-210:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/77520.html), the [Design Council](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/), the [GOV.UK Government Design Principles](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/government-design-principles) (updated 2 April 2025), and [ISO 9241-110:2020](https://www.iso.org/standard/75258.html). This index is not a claim of compliance.

## Precedence

Apply the first principle that can settle the conflict. A lower one never overrides a higher one.

1. [Protect before you enable](../../principle-protect-before-you-enable/SKILL.md)
2. [Start from the outcome](../../principle-start-from-the-outcome/SKILL.md)
3. [Include the hardest realistic case](../../principle-include-the-hardest-case/SKILL.md)
4. [See the whole service, then do less](../../principle-see-the-whole-service/SKILL.md)
5. [Take on the hard work](../../principle-take-on-the-hard-work/SKILL.md)
6. [Behave predictably and recoverably](../../principle-behave-predictably/SKILL.md)
7. [Spend less of the world](../../principle-spend-less-of-the-world/SKILL.md)

The next five govern the quality of the specification. They never override the seven above.

8. [Design every state](../../principle-design-every-state/SKILL.md)
9. [Make it testable](../../principle-make-it-testable/SKILL.md)
10. [One source of truth](../../principle-one-source-of-truth/SKILL.md)
11. [Decide the reversible](../../principle-decide-the-reversible/SKILL.md)
12. [Use real content](../../principle-use-real-content/SKILL.md)

Polish, novelty, and satisfaction yield to all of the above. Name the principle that decided the trade-off and the principle it cost. If the evidence cannot support the higher principle, the gap stays unknown.

"Make things open" is a GOV.UK operating rule for public work. Apply it when the product is a public service or the human asked for it.
