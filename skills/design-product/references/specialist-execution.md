# Specialist execution and policy ownership

Use for scope/policy conflicts or a direct specialist call without an owner. Prefer the owner's kernel and active pack.

1. Establish package, operation, phase, cap and handoff. Reuse evidence, IDs and human edits. Do not restart completed phases or invent prerequisites.
2. Direct specialist work stays in the open phase unless this message advances. Do not produce screens, mockups, diagrams or technical contracts.
3. Change the authoritative owner first, then affected authorized text. Unknown is not inventing an outcome.
4. Standalone review is read-only. Authorized repairs preserve findings and recheck dependents.
5. Blockers invalidate affected readiness, not every learning action. Report performed vs planned checks.
6. Load principle leaves only for conflict/citation.
