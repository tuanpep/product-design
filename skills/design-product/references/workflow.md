# Workflow and gates

Compatibility stub. Prefer [execution kernel](execution-kernel.md) and [phase routing](phase-routing.md).

Phases: Understand → Shape (documents) → Specify (Full only). Advance opens the next phase; Full/advise alone do not. Levels: `understand` | `design-documents` | `full`. Default Shape after advance: design-documents.

Essentials artifacts: decision brief `product-design.md`; `test-plan.md` at Shape; SRS `build-spec.md` only at Full Specify. [Documentero selection](documentero-templates.md) owns templates and single ownership. Test cases belong in the plan; the requirements matrix belongs in SRS Appendices. No screens, mockups, diagrams or technical design.

Discovery policy: [discovery-interview.md](discovery-interview.md). Learning: [learning-loop.md](learning-loop.md). Principles: [principles.md](principles.md).
